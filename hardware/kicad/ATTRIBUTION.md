# Reversible footprint attribution

`arc58-reversible.pretty/Sofle_Choc_Hotswap_SK6812MINI-E_Reversible.kicad_mod`
adapts the combined Choc-hotswap / SK6812MINI-E reversible geometry from
[Sofle Choc](https://github.com/josefadamcik/SofleKeyboard), Copyright (c)
2019 Josef Adamcik, under the MIT License. The original license is reproduced
in the upstream repository. Arc 58 changes only the modern KiCad syntax and
the assembly text; the hole, socket-pad, LED-pad, and LED-aperture geometry is
preserved.

The controller and reversible TRRS topology were cross-checked against
[Lily58](https://github.com/kata0510/Lily58), also MIT licensed.
